## **LES 4 PRINCIPES FONDAMENTAUX**

## **Principe \#1 : MEDDIC Qualification Drives Everything**

**Règle :** Before you demo, you MUST qualify all 6 MEDDIC elements.

text  
`M - Metrics       : Quelles métriques améliorer ?`  
`E - Economic Buyer : Qui contrôle le budget ?`  
`D - Decision Criteria : Quels critères pour choisir ?`  
`D - Decision Process : Qui approuve, dans quel ordre ?`  
`I - Identify Pain : Quel problème = existentiel ?`  
`C - Champion       : Qui defend votre solution ?`

**If ANY element is missing → DO NOT DEMO.**

---

## **Principe \#2 : Different Stakeholders \= Different Demos**

**Les 4 Rôles Clés :**

| Rôle | Cares About | Duration | Demo Focus |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Economic Buyer (CFO)** | ROI, Budget, Risk | 30 min | Business outcomes |
| **Technical Buyer (CTO)** | Integration, Security, Scale | 60 min | Architecture |
| **User Buyer (VP Sales)** | Usability, Workflow, Adoption | 45 min | Daily experience |
| **Compliance (Legal/Procurement)** | Terms, Compliance, Audit | 20 min | Certifications |

**Bad:** Same presentation for all  
**Good:** Tailored presentation per role

---

## **Principe \#3 : Champion-Led Strategy is Cornerstone**

**Champion Definition :**

* Has power (VP level+)  
* Has pain (personal motivation)  
* Has political capital (can influence peers)  
* Willing to advocate (explicitly confirmed)

**Impact :** With champion: 60% close rate. Without: 25% close rate.

Your job: Coach champion to advocate internally.

---

## **Principe \#4 : Business Case is Mandatory**

**Every enterprise deal needs written business case** (not just slides).

**Contents :**

1. Executive Summary (1 page)  
2. Financial Model (ROI, payback, 3-year projection)  
3. Implementation Timeline (Gantt chart)  
4. Risk Assessment & Mitigation  
5. Success Metrics (post-sale measurement)

**Why :** CFO needs to justify to Finance Committee. Provides accountability.

## **TOP 10 ENTERPRISE OBJECTIONS**

| \# | Objection | Response Strategy |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **1** | "We need to evaluate other vendors" | Use Decision Criteria to differentiate vs competitors |
| **2** | "Your price is 2x Competitor X" | TCO analysis (include THEIR implementation costs \+ ours) |
| **3** | "We need board approval" | Build board-ready business case for CFO to present |
| **4** | "Need security sign-off from CISO" | Provide SOC 2/GDPR/HIPAA docs \+ offer CISO call |

| **10** | "Want to check with your other customers" | Provide 3 references \+ suggest questions to ask |

**Objection Handling Process (Universal) :**

1. Listen fully  
2. Validate their concern ("That's legitimate...")  
3. Tell relevant customer story  
4. Provide your answer  
5. Verify objection resolved ("Any lingering concerns?")
